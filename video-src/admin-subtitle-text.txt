Every clinic asks me the same question: can our team update the website without calling a developer? With this admin panel, yes. Your clinic manager logs in, and everything is in one menu: Reception. On Clinic details and hours, you change the phone number, email and address in one place. Opening hours are set day by day. Pick the times, or tick Closed. Need to tell patients something, like a holiday closing? Write a short notice and choose the dates. It shows on every page, and disappears by itself. Mistakes are caught. If the closing time is before the opening time, nothing is saved, and the page says what to fix. Click Save, and you get a clear confirmation. The website updates straight away: the notice at the top, the hours in the header and footer, and the details Google reads. The Doctors list shows who works which days, and whether online booking is on. Open a doctor to change their working hours, add a split shift, or mark a day off. Online booking only offers those times. For holidays, training days or leave, add a closure, and those times disappear from booking at once. Reception staff can manage appointments, but they can't change these settings. Only the manager can. If your clinic needs a website your own team can manage, send me a message.
