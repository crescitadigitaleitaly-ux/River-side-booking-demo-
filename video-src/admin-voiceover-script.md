# Voice-over: "Your team can update the website" (admin panel walkthrough)

Voice: Mouzzam's clone (voice_id ceEarI8D5RvxxVecxOIJ). Rules: demo clinic, fictional data, no platform names, CTA "send me a message".
Marker = first words of each part (used to time the pictures). Screens: `video/admin/shots/*.png` (scripts/admin-shots.mjs).

[intro] title card
Every clinic asks me the same question: can our team update the website without calling a developer? With this admin panel, yes.

[menu] 01-menu
Your clinic manager logs in, and everything is in one menu: Reception.

[contact] 02-contact
On Clinic details and hours, you change the phone number, email and address in one place.

[hours] 03-hours
Opening hours are set day by day. Pick the times, or tick Closed.

[notice] 04-notice
Need to tell patients something, like a holiday closing? Write a short notice and choose the dates. It shows on every page, and disappears by itself.

[error] 05-error
Mistakes are caught. If the closing time is before the opening time, nothing is saved, and the page says what to fix.

[saved] 06-saved
Click Save, and you get a clear confirmation.

[site] 07-site + 08-footer
The website updates straight away: the notice at the top, the hours in the header and footer, and the details Google reads.

[doctors] 09-doctors
The Doctors list shows who works which days, and whether online booking is on.

[doctorhours] 10-doctor-hours
Open a doctor to change their working hours, add a split shift, or mark a day off. Online booking only offers those times.

[closures] 11-closures
For holidays, training days or leave, add a closure, and those times disappear from booking at once.

[roles] 12-reception-blocked
Reception staff can manage appointments, but they can't change these settings. Only the manager can.

[outro] outro card
If your clinic needs a website your own team can manage, send me a message.
